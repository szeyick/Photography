Execution: Pinpoint Tracking Strike

Active Hardware Mechanics

⚙️ Left Top Dial: Film Profiles

Turn to NC for Classic Negative look or A for default Monochrome profiles.

Set to C for moving tracks. Avoid leaving on S if targets shift distance rapidly.
